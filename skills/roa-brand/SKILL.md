# ROA Off-Road Brand Rules

Follow every rule below for any ROA work. If a rule and a request conflict, ask before breaking the rule.

## 1. Writing: the Obvious Adams test

Every line must pass this test:

1. What plain fact does this sentence exist to say?
2. Say that fact in the simplest words possible.
3. Would a 10-year-old understand it on the first read? If not, rewrite it.

Rules:
- Short sentences.
- Numbers over adjectives.
- The words a normal buyer already uses, not industry words.
- Turn every spec into a benefit a person can picture.
  - Weak: "20kWh lithium battery."
  - Strong: "20kWh of battery. That runs the A/C all night with no hookups."
- If in doubt, simpler wins.

Banned:
- Flowery or poetic language.
- Clever wordplay and puns.
- Metaphors that decorate instead of explain.
- Dramatic buildups.
- Any sentence that sounds impressive but adds no fact.

## 2. Voice

Confident. Genuine. Expert. Simple.

- Confident: state facts. No hedging, no hype.
- Genuine: only claim what is true.
- Expert: get specs exactly right.
- Simple: see the Obvious Adams test above.

## 3. Vocabulary

| Use | Never use |
|---|---|
| Roamers | customers, buyers, clients |
| Future Roamers | prospects, leads |
| Team Members | employees, staff |
| Experience Centers | dealership, dealer, lot, showroom |
| Sales Coaches | salespeople, sales reps |
| Leaders | managers, bosses |

## 4. Positioning

- Premium.
- Transformation over transaction: sell the life the Roamer gets, not the deal.
- Blue ocean: sell ROA on its own merits. Do not compare to competitors.

| Use | Font |
|---|---|
| Title | Rajdhani Bold, ALL CAPS |
| Headlines H1–H3 | Rajdhani (Bold, SemiBold, or Regular by level) |
