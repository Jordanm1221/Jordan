## VIDEO CONCEPTS

---

### VIDEO 1: "We Put Our Name On It" — Brand Launch Film (60–90 sec)

**Tone:** Cinematic. Proud. Confident. Zero hype, all substance.

**Concept:**
Open on ROA's logo being placed on the Roamer X exterior — a physical badge being installed. No narration yet. Just the sound of metal, wind, quiet.

Then a single voice (founder/CEO):
> *"We've sold a lot of trailers. We've recommended a lot of builds. But this one is different. This one has our name on it."*

Cut through each pillar visually — no talking, just footage:
- Power: Solar panels unfolding, lithium readout screen, generator kicking on in the dark
- Comfort: Fireplace flickering, king bed made up, leather recliners, projector dropping
- Capability: Trailer navigating rough terrain, air suspension adjusting, MOLLE panel loaded up

End card:
> **Roamer X. Founders Edition. 50 units. ROA.**
> [Reserve Now]

---

### VIDEO 2: "Three Pillars" — Spec Deep Dive (3 x 30-sec series or 90-sec full cut)

**Tone:** Direct. Technical. Confident. Buyers who care about specs will love this.

**Concept:** Split into three 30-second pillars, each deployable standalone or stitched together.

**PILLAR 1 — Xtraordinary Power (30 sec)**
> Open: Dark campsite. One button press. Everything powers on.
>
> VO: *"3,900 watts of solar. 20 kilowatt-hours of Epoch lithium. A 6,000-watt LP generator backing it up. Run your dishwasher. Your washer/dryer. Both A/C units. In the middle of nowhere, with zero grid hookup. That's Xtraordinary Power."*
>
> End card: XTRAORDINARY POWER | ROAMER X

**PILLAR 2 — Xceptional Comfort (30 sec)**
> Open: Interior shot — fireplace on, rain on the roof, coffee being made.
>
> VO: *"King bed. Leather recliners. Fireplace. Private bathroom. Dual-zone A/C. Washer/dryer. Projector screen. 32-inch Smart TV. Induction stove. This isn't a trailer you camp in. It's a four-season home you bring with you. That's Xceptional Comfort."*
>
> End card: XCEPTIONAL COMFORT | ROAMER X

**PILLAR 3 — Xtreme Capability (30 sec)**
> Open: Truck and trailer rolling over rough off-road terrain.
>
> VO: *"Full aluminum frame. Monoframe air suspension. 12 brake drums. 1,000 GVWR. Built in America by Pause. Patio at the push of a button. Rock lights. MOLLE panels. Integrated floor drain. This unit was built to go — and it doesn't stop when the road does. That's Xtreme Capability."*
>
> End card: XTREME CAPABILITY | ROAMER X

### VIDEO 4: "Day in the Life" — Off-Grid Lifestyle (60–90 sec)

**Tone:** Aspirational but grounded. Real use, real capability.

**Concept:**
Follow one person/couple through a full off-grid day with the Roamer X. No voiceover — just ambient sound and music.

**Shot list:**
- Morning: Coffee on the induction stove. Fireplace on. Misty mountains outside.
- Patio extends with a button press. Camp chair drops, morning view.
- Power check on the lithium readout — full charge from overnight solar.
- Midday: Running the washer/dryer. Dishwasher going. A/C set. Projector pulled down for midday movie.
- Afternoon: Hiking out. Trailer sitting solid on rough ground — air suspension shown.
- Evening: Rock lights on, MOLLE panel with gear organized, outdoor patio bug screen up.
- Night: Leather recliners, 32" Smart TV, fireplace. King bed made.

End card: **This is the Roamer X. 50 Founders Edition units.** [Reserve Yours]
