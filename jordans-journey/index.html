    ABOUT

    I'm Jordan. I drive trails, film them, and photograph them.

    This site is my trail log. Each trail gets a pin on the map, a difficulty rating from 1 to 10, the truck setup you need, and the video and photos from my run.

    Trail conditions change with weather and season. Check with the local land office before you go, and never drive a hard trail alone.
